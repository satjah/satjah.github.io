Satja Hongyok

สัจจ หงษ์หยก · Aod

A fourth-generation Phuket son who never planned to be anything in particular, was called home in 2013, and became whatever the family needed next.

I was born in 1981 in Ku Ku and grew up in Phuket town. I studied computer science at SIIT and AIT, then spent eight years building systems for a bank. I never meant to come home.

In 2013 my grandfather called. Since then I have been whatever the family needed: salesman, finance, IT, cook by necessity, photographer by necessity, speaker by necessity. I am not gifted, so I do more.

A house my great-grandfather built in 1936 had stood empty for over thirty years. My brother and I spent three years restoring it as a gift to our grandfather. The house is 90 this year, so this record has two layers — the house's and mine.

I have worn the same black-and-white clothes every day for seven or eight years, about as long as the restoration took. This site is black and white for the same reason. It is not a style I picked.

Not gifted, so I do more.
